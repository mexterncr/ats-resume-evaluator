Process Architecture

How It Works in 4 Steps

1

Upload Resume

2

Job Description

Target role scope, qualifications, and core requirements are provided.

3

NLP Vectorization

Stopwords are eliminated, tokens regularized, and TF-IDF bi-gram matrix generated.

4

Detailed Report

Final multi-weighted score and actionable keyword gaps are delivered.
